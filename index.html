Tier 1Chemistry & materials · Rheology

Constitutive protocol information

constitutive-protocol-information

Design three rheology experiments that reveal the most information about a hidden material’s relaxation spectrum.

↗Reference

Adaptive and Bayesian experimental design for constitutive models.

Villarreal et al. 2023 ↗ Bhattacharya, Cao & Stuart 2026 ↗

Inputs

  • A complex-modulus model with a baseline, three Maxwell branches, and six unknown parameters.
  • Comparator source code, a local checker, and development cases with known answers.
  • Per hidden case: two initial frequency observations of the real and imaginary modulus with Gaussian noise, an allowed-protocol menu, and three queries. Each query returns one protocol’s noisy observation.
  • 64 fresh cases per evaluation; hidden parameters are withheld.
2 hours4 vCPU8 GB RAM

Task

Build an adaptive method that chooses frequency protocols to maximise information about a three-branch Maxwell relaxation spectrum, outperforming three design baselines given the same information.

Submit main.py. For each case, issue exactly three distinct protocol queries, then return a positive, finite six-parameter estimate.

What is measuredThe score measures the information gathered by the experiments. The final parameter estimate is checked for validity.

Verification

Metric
Negative log-determinant of the Fisher information matrix, evaluated at the true parameters over the initial and selected measurements, with a small ridge. Lower loss is better.
Comparators
A fixed prior-linearised design, a sequential Fisher-information design, and an importance-weighted posterior design. All use the same cases and available information.
Pass criteria
A one-sided exact sign test with p < 0.05/3 against each of the three comparators, plus a positive mean gain against each.
Validity
Correct output shape, positive and finite estimates, and exactly three distinct allowed protocols per case. Evaluation runs in an isolated environment.
Tier 2Clinical & health sciences · Cardiac electrophysiology

Optical mapping activation maps

optical-mapping-activation-maps

Turn a raw camera recording of beating cardiac tissue into activation-time and action-potential-duration maps that match an expert’s analysis.

↗Reference

Delshad, Cherry et al. 2023 ↗

The Journal of Physiology. Method reference, with an unpublished Fenton-lab recording from Georgia Tech processed by the lab’s expert.

Inputs

  • One 250 MB, 16-bit recording: 128 × 128 pixels, 529.09 frames/s, and 7,620 frames spanning 18 beats.
  • A vendor header and per-frame layout. The stream is transposed, frame 0 is under-exposed, and fluorescence polarity is undocumented.
  • Fixed definitions for activation time (interpolated 50% upstroke crossing), APD80, and beat detection.
  • A Python scientific stack, without internet access. Expert maps and the tissue mask are withheld.
2 hours4 vCPU16 GB RAM

Task

Segment the tissue and recover its electrical activation and action-potential duration at 80% repolarisation (APD80), averaging over usable beats.

Submit three 128 × 128 maps: a tissue mask, activation time in milliseconds, and APD80 in milliseconds. Include the reproducible analysis pipeline.

Provide a methods summary covering the approach, what the method targets, validation performed, budget used, and limitations.

Verification

Metric
Activation-map RMSE against the expert map within the intersection of the masks, after removing each map’s median offset. APD80 RMSE is a gated secondary metric.
Mask quality
Cover at least 85% of the expert’s tissue, with intersection over union (IoU) ≥ 0.55. Hidden gates check the mask outline and the smoothness of both maps.
Pass criteria
Activation error below one camera frame (1.89 ms), APD80 error below two frames (3.78 ms), all mask and smoothness gates satisfied, and the methods file present.
Integrity checks
The deterministic grader reads submitted arrays without executing submitted code. Validity probes include label permutation, the wrong upstroke definition, reversed polarity, and a missing transpose.
Tier 3Life sciences · Metagenomics

Strain-resolved assembly

strain-resolved-assembly

Assemble closely related bacterial strains from mixed long reads, preserving long-range allele linkage and improving on frozen assembly baselines.

↗Reference

Complete RefSeq chromosomes of nonpathogenic strains, including E. coli K-12 MG1655 and BL21(DE3) at 99.2% identity.

Reads are simulated with pbsim3: ONT R10-like, approximately 99% accurate, and 12 kb long. Reference assemblers are myloasm, metaMDBG, and metaFlye.

Inputs

  • Five development mixtures with reads, mixture metadata, full truth genomes and read origins, plus a re-simulator. Strain counts, abundances, and species are undisclosed in the mixture metadata.
  • myloasm, metaMDBG, Flye, minimap2, samtools, seqkit, and racon; comparator code, development scores, and evaluation code.
  • Hidden evaluation: 15 mixtures from genomes absent from development, totalling about 1.7 Gb of reads.
  • Five regimes span 97–99.9% identity, 1:1–6:1 abundance, shared repeats, a recombinant mosaic, and real strain pairs.
8 hours16 vCPU64 GB RAM

Task

Build a pipeline that separates mixtures of two or three closely related strains into correctly phased haplotypes, retaining long-range allele linkage.

Submit run.py to produce, for each mixture, phased haplotypes (FASTA), an assembly graph (GFA), read assignments, and per-contig phase confidence.

Include method.json, a methods summary, and an executable ablation variant.

Verification

Metric
Contigs are aligned to hidden truth genomes and split at misjoins and phase switches; each truth interval is credited once. Utility rewards long, correctly phased segments using squared segment lengths normalised by strain length, averaged over strains and mixtures.
Quality gates
Limits on falsely joined bases, phase switches per Mb, duplication, false contigs, and unaligned sequence, with consensus quality QV ≥ 30.
Pass criteria
The paired stratified bootstrap lower bound on utility gain must exceed a preset margin against every frozen assembler comparator: default and tuned myloasm, metaMDBG, and metaFlye. It must also be positive against packaged phasing baselines and an assembler-tuning control, with every quality gate satisfied.
Evaluation
Submitted code is replayed offline under an unprivileged user on the hidden mixtures, within the fixed compute budget.
